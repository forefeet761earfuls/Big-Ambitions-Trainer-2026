## 🎮 About the Game

Big Ambitions is a role-playing business simulation game developed and published by Hovgaard Games. It entered Early Access on March 10, 2023, and reached full release on August 28, 2026. Set in an open-world version of New York City, the game combines entrepreneurship, management, logistics, real estate, and life-simulation mechanics.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Big Ambitions**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for managing money, personal needs, time, inventory, vehicles, and business operations in Big Ambitions. It is designed for players who want to experiment with business layouts, accelerate progression, reduce routine management, or freely explore the city and its economic systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to test business strategies, build large commercial networks, explore the city without routine limitations, create experimental save files, or streamline repetitive economic and life-simulation tasks.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Money** — Maintain a chosen cash balance for purchases, rent, loans, vehicles, property, furniture, stock, and business expenses.
- **Freeze Personal Needs** — Prevent sleep, hunger, health, and happiness requirements from decreasing while the option is active.
- **Restore Personal Needs** — Restore the character's health, happiness, energy, and other personal needs to comfortable levels.
- **Freeze Time** — Pause the passage of in-game time while retaining control over the character and management interfaces.
- **Fast Game Speed** — Accelerate routine activities, travel, production, deliveries, and business operating cycles.
- **Business Stock Control** — Adjust available business inventory to simplify restocking shops, offices, warehouses, and other commercial properties.

- **Easy Logistics** — Assist with supply movement between suppliers, warehouses, businesses, vehicles, and distribution networks.

- **Unlimited Inventory** — Reduce or remove practical limits when carrying and handling items used for personal needs, businesses, and deliveries.

### 🌍 World

- **Unlimited Vehicle Fuel** — Keep owned vehicles supplied with fuel during city travel and business errands.

### ⚔️ Combat

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Big Ambitions uses direct character control and city travel through vehicles and public transportation. Use the trainer after loading into the game and activate movement-related options only when the character is fully spawned. Unlimited fuel is useful for extended driving, while time control can make long journeys and errands easier to manage.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory and stock options are intended for items carried by the character, stored in vehicles, or used by businesses. Add or adjust supplies only while the relevant inventory or business screen is open. Avoid changing quantities during an active delivery, purchase, sale, or warehouse transfer to reduce the risk of desynchronizing the save.

### World & Environment

The city is an interactive business sandbox where buildings can be rented or purchased, businesses can be furnished and stocked, and vehicles can be bought for transportation and logistics. Money, time, fuel, and personal-needs options make it easier to explore neighborhoods, test property investments, and develop supply networks without pausing to manage every routine expense.

### Combat & Weapons

Big Ambitions has no combat gameplay. There are no weapons, enemy encounters, combat statistics, or fighting missions. The trainer therefore focuses on business management, personal needs, transportation, inventory, logistics, and progression rather than combat enhancements.
